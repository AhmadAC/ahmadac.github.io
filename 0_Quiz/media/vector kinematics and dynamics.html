Vector Kinematics and Dynamics

矢量运动学与动力学

Foundation: Dynamics and Kinematics | 基础概念：动力学与运动学

Term 1: Dynamics | 动力学

Dynamics is the branch of physics that studies why objects move. It examines how forces like pushes, pulls, gravity, and water resistance cause an object to speed up, slow down, stop, or turn.

动力学是物理学中研究物体为什么运动的分支。它专门研究推力、拉力、重力和流体阻力等各种受力如何导致物体加速、减速、静止或改变方向。

Term 2: Vector Kinematics | 矢量运动学

Kinematics is the branch of physics that describes how objects move without focusing on the forces causing the motion. Vector Kinematics uses directional mathematical measurements (such as position, displacement, velocity, and acceleration) to describe the exact path and speed of that motion.

运动学是只描述物体如何运动而不探究其受力原因的学科。矢量运动学则是利用带有方向性的测量量（例如位置、位移、速度和加速度），精确描述物体的运动轨迹与快慢。

1. Force | 力

Definition | 定义

A force is a push or pull exerted upon an object resulting from its interaction with another object. Force is quantified using the standard International System (SI) unit named the newton (N).

力是物体之间相互作用所产生的推力或拉力。在国际单位制（SI）中，力的标准度量单位为牛顿（N）。

When a paddle boat operates, its rotating blades push backward against the water. The water exerts an equal reaction force that propels the hull forward. Simultaneously, the forward motion of the hull is resisted by fluid drag.

当明轮船运转时，旋转的桨叶向后推水。水体随之施加一个大小相等的反作用力，推动船体向前行进。同时，船体的前进运动受到流体阻力的阻碍。

2. Magnitude | 大小（数值）

Definition | 定义

Magnitude refers strictly to the numerical size, amount, or physical quantity of a measurement. Magnitude is expressed by a number followed by an appropriate unit, independent of direction.

大小（数值）严格指代某个测量值的数值大小或物理量值。它由数值及相应单位组成，不包含任何方向信息。

In scientific vector illustrations, magnitude is represented by the physical length of the arrow. A longer arrow denotes a greater magnitude.

在科学矢量图示中，大小由箭头的实际长度表示。箭头越长，表示物理量的数值越大。

3. Scalar and Vector Quantities | 标量与矢量

Definition | 定义
  • A scalar quantity has magnitude only. It is completely described by a number and a unit.
  • 标量只有大小，没有方向。仅凭数值和单位即可完整表述。
  • A vector quantity has both magnitude and direction. Both properties are required to specify it.
  • 矢量同时具备大小和方向两个要素。必须同时指明二者才能完整描述。

4. Distance and Displacement | 路程与位移

Definition | 定义
  • Distance (Scalar): The total length of the continuous path traveled by an object, accounting for every turn.
  • 路程（标量）：物体实际运动轨迹的总长度，包含所有曲线与转折。
  • Displacement (Vector): The shortest straight-line length and direction pointing directly from the starting position to the ending position.
  • 位移（矢量）：从起始位置直接指向终点位置的最短有向线段，包含直线距离与空间方向。

5. Sum of Forces (Net Force) | 合力

Definition | 定义

The sum of forces, also termed net force (denoted as ΣF), is the overall vector combination of all individual forces acting upon an object. When forces act along the same straight line, forces in the positive direction are added, while opposing forces are subtracted.

合力（通常记作 ΣF）是指作用在同一物体上的所有外力的矢量和。当多个力在同一直线上作用时，同方向的力相加，反方向的力相减。
